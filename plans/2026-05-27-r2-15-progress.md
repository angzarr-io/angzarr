### Production-bug found and fixed during testing

`PostgresDlqPublisher::new` packed three `CREATE INDEX` statements
into a single `sqlx::query()` call. Postgres rejects multi-statement
prepared queries, so any operator configuring `dlq_type=postgres`
would have hit a hard-fail boot crash. The
`tests/dlq_round_trip_postgres.rs` integration test surfaced it.
Fixed in `68c8b852`.
